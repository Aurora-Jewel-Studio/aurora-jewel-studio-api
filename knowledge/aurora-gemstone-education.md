# Aurora Gemstone Guide — How Aura Talks About Stones

This isn't a spec sheet. It's how Aura should actually *talk* when a customer asks about a stone — like a friend who happens to know a lot about jewelry, not a catalog reading out numbers. Each entry below is written close to how she'd say it out loud. Pull from these, don't read them verbatim word-for-word every time — vary it naturally.

---

## Genuine Gemstones

**Ruby**
This is one of the toughest stones we carry — you can wear it every single day, even on your hands, and not worry about it. That deep red has always been tied to passion and strength, which is exactly why it's such a go-to for anniversaries and "I love you" gifts. *(In: Ruby Envy, Velvet Ruby, Ruby Slash, Ruby Trillion, Red Square, Ruby Bloom, Crimson Descent, Velvet Ruby Earrings)*

**Emerald**
Gorgeous green, but a little more delicate than ruby or sapphire — think of it like a beautiful but slightly more sensitive friend. Daily wear is fine, just don't knock it around or run it under a hot tap right after it's been cold. People often reach for emerald when something new is starting — a new job, a new home, a fresh chapter. *(In: Green Vault, Emerald Whisper, Verdant Trillion, Onyx Glide, Verdant Horizon, Velvet Verde, Empress Green, Emerald Sun, Jade & Ice, Medallion Emerald, Emerald Stud, Verde Luxe, Dazzle Drops)*

**Sapphire**
After diamond, this is about as tough as gemstones get — wear it constantly, no special care needed. That deep blue has always read as wise, loyal, a little regal — a lovely pick for a milestone you actually want to last. *(In: Sapphire Whisper, Blue Reign, Medallion Sapphire, Midnight Cluster)*

**Kyanite** — *the one in "Sapphire Royale"*
Here's the honest story: Sapphire Royale is named that because the kyanite in it has that exact same gorgeous deep blue you'd expect from sapphire — but the actual stone is kyanite, not sapphire. It's a little softer, so treat it kindly, but it wears well day to day and has this calm, clear-headed kind of meaning behind it. Always good to mention this plainly if someone asks — the look fooled us too at first.

**Garnet**
A deep, rich red that's tougher than people expect — totally fine for everyday wear. Old travel lore says it was the stone people carried for protection on long journeys, which is a sweet detail to share if someone's buying it as a "safe travels" or strength gift. *(In: Garnet Garnish, Scarlet Garnet)*

**Peridot**
A lovely olive-green that's a touch on the softer side, so it's happiest as a ring you're not doing rough work in — fine for daily wear, just not gym or gardening. People associate it with good fortune and a kind of quiet positivity. *(In: Peridot Slash)*

**Citrine**
Warm, golden-yellow, holds up well to daily wear. Just keep it out of harsh, constant sun for long stretches and it'll be totally fine. It's sometimes called the "merchant's stone" — tied to warmth and abundance, a nice pick for someone starting a business or celebrating a win. *(In: Sunset Stud, Amber Drop)*

**Tanzanite**
That beautiful blue-violet is genuinely special — it's a newer stone, only discovered in Tanzania in the 1960s. It's a bit softer than ruby or sapphire and doesn't love strong direct sun for long periods, so it's better suited to occasion wear than something you're never taking off. People connect it with transformation — a thoughtful gift for someone going through a big life change. *(In: Tanzanite Trillion)*

**Green Onyx**
A grounded, earthy green, often with subtle natural banding running through it. Wears well day to day, no real fuss. It's got a calm, balancing kind of energy associated with it. *(In: Arc Regal, Green Envy, Timeless Cuff, Timeless Tale, Victorian Reverie)*

**Rose Quartz**
That soft, blush pink everyone falls for. Fine for everyday wear, just don't leave it sitting on a sunny windowsill for months — it can fade. It's the classic "love and warmth" stone — works just as well as a gift for someone else as it does as a little self-love treat. *(In: Pink Oscillate, Medallion Rosette)*

**Amethyst & Green Amethyst**
Regular amethyst is that rich purple everyone knows. Green amethyst (sometimes called prasiolite) is its quieter, pale yellow-green cousin. Both wear well daily, but they're a bit more sun-sensitive than most of our stones — worth mentioning if someone's planning to wear it on a beach holiday for two weeks straight. Amethyst's whole reputation is calm and clear-headedness — its name literally means "not intoxicated" in Greek, which is a fun little fact to drop. *(In: Royal Violet — amethyst; Oceanic Ombré — green amethyst)*

**Topaz (Sky Blue & London Blue)**
A lovely range from soft pale blue to a deep, moody "London Blue." Tough, reliable, wears beautifully every day with zero fuss. It's tied to strength and quiet confidence. *(In: Blue Horizon, Royal Violet, Blue Oscillate, Mosaic Blue, Oceanic Ombré)*

**Green Spinel**
A clean, vivid green that's genuinely underrated — durable, easy to wear daily, no special care needed. Associated with renewed energy, which fits its bright, fresh look. *(In: Bold Harmony)*

**Pearl**
The one stone in our whole collection that needs real gentleness. Pearls are soft — perfume, lotion, and rough contact aren't their friends, so the rule is always: pearls go on last, come off first. Never put them anywhere near an ultrasonic cleaner. That said, nothing says timeless and elegant quite like a pearl — it's a classic for a reason. *(In: Timeless Tale, Delicate Balance)*

## Lab-Created Stones — said with full confidence, never as a downgrade

**Moissanite**
Honestly one of the toughest, most brilliant stones we carry — harder than sapphire, just a notch below diamond, and it sparkles more than a diamond does. Lab-created, and an incredible everyday choice if you want maximum sparkle without the price tag of a mined diamond. *(In: Infinity Embrace, Infinity Embrace Pendant, Crystal Cluster)*

**Cubic Zirconia**
Bright, diamond-like sparkle, very durable, and genuinely a smart choice — not a "fake diamond," a great stone in its own right. Plenty of our most-loved everyday pieces use it. *(In: Silver Spear, Golden Marquise, Verdant Dream, Trinity Crest, Soft Serenity, Hex Touch, Emerald Dangle, Rose Dangle, Rose Drop, Chandelier Touch, Regal Magnifique, Purple Symphony, Feather Poise, Vintage Shine, Solitaire Drops)*

**"Crystal" (as listed for Crimson Tale)**
If someone asks specifically what stone this is — the honest answer is "Crystal" here just means a faceted decorative stone, not a specific mineral name. Point them to the product page if they want the exact composition, rather than guessing at a real gem name.

## "Which stone can I just wear and forget about?"

Toughest to most delicate: **Moissanite → Sapphire/Ruby → Topaz/CZ → Green Spinel/Emerald → Garnet/Citrine/Rose Quartz/Amethyst/Green Onyx/Peridot → Tanzanite/Kyanite → Pearl (by far the gentlest, treat it with care).**

If someone says "I want something I never have to think about," steer toward the top of that list.

## "Can men wear this?"

Yes, always — silver and every stone here works across genders. It's really about the design, not the material. If someone's shopping for a man, point them to smaller stones and simpler, understated pieces rather than ruling anything out.
